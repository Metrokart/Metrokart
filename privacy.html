Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase something from our store, we collect personal information such as your name, address, and email address as part of the buying and selling process.

Additionally, when you browse our website, we automatically receive your device’s Internet Protocol (IP) address to help us understand your browser and operating system for better user experience.

With your permission, we may also send you emails, SMS, WhatsApp messages, RCS, or call you regarding:

  • Orders and service updates
  • New products
  • Promotional offers

2. Consent

How do we obtain your consent?
When you provide personal information to complete a transaction, verify your payment, place an order, arrange delivery, or return a purchase, we consider that you consent to our collecting and using it for that purpose.

For secondary uses like marketing, we will either:

  • Ask for your explicit consent, or
  • Provide you an option to opt out

How to withdraw consent?
You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at:

We may disclose your personal information if:

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party service providers (such as payment gateways) to process transactions. These providers will only collect, use, and disclose your information as necessary to perform their services.

Please note:

  • These providers have their own privacy policies
  • Your data may be subject to laws of different jurisdictions

Once you leave our website or are redirected to a third-party platform, this Privacy Policy no longer applies.

Our website may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and recommend reviewing their policies.

6. Security

We take reasonable precautions and follow industry best practices to protect your personal information from:

  • Loss
  • Misuse

  • Disclosure or alteration

7. Cookies

We use cookies to maintain your session on our website. These cookies do not personally identify you on other websites.

8. Age of Consent

By using this site, you confirm that:

  • You are at least the age of majority in your state/province, or

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will take effect immediately upon posting on this page.
